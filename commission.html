Common questions.

Do you work with clients outside New Mexico, Texas, and Arizona?

Yes. I deliver locally, and delivery is available throughout the U.S. Tell me where you are and we'll work out the details in your quote.

How long does a commission take?

It depends on the piece. I never compromise quality for time, and I give every client a realistic schedule that works with their life.

Can I bring my own design or sketch?

Yes. Whether it's the type of wood, the finish, the size, or a design detail, I'll work closely with you to bring your idea to life.

Do you offer a guarantee?

Yes. I only work with the best materials available and provide a one-year guarantee on all labor.

Do you do repairs?

Yes. I've repaired, reinforced, and restored chairs, tables, and furniture damaged in moves. Call to talk about your piece.
