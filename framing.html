Framing questions.

What can you frame?

Artwork, photography, and heirlooms. If you have something unusual, call and describe it, and we'll talk through how to frame it.

What are the frames made of?

Each frame is hand-joined from hardwood in my Chamberino workshop. The wood and finish are chosen with you, so a frame can match the piece, the room, or furniture you already own.

Is the work guaranteed?

Yes. I provide a one-year guarantee on all labor.
