Quick answers.

How quickly do you respond?

I reply to emails and voicemails as soon as I can. If I'm in the middle of a build or out on a delivery, it may take a little longer.

Do I need an appointment to visit the shop?

Yes. Workshop visits are by appointment only. Call or email ahead and we'll pick a time.

Where do you deliver?

I deliver locally, and delivery is available throughout the U.S. See service areas for the cities I serve most often.

Can I send you a photo of the room?

Please do. A photo of the room, a rough measurement or two, and a note about what the piece needs to do is enough for me to start sketching. Email works best for attachments.
